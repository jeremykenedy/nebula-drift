# Artwork and screenshot provenance

The nebula is drawn at runtime by `NebulaRenderer` using a procedural OpenGL ES
fragment shader. It includes no bundled photographs, video, fonts, or third-party
visual assets. The icon, TV banner, and DreamService preview are original vector
resources in this repository.

`docs/screenshots/nebula-violet.png` is a direct ADB screen capture from the
Google Android TV API 31 emulator at 1920 x 1080 after at least 40 seconds of
animation. It was not retouched. The image
`art/screensaver-preview.jpg` is a 384 x 216 JPEG resized from that capture for
screensaver-picker thumbnails. It keeps the rendered scene and has no title,
overlay, logo, or watermark.

The settings image `docs/screenshots/settings.png` is a direct ADB capture from
the same emulator and documents the remote-operated settings screen. Emulator
screenshots are not evidence of physical display resolution or performance.
